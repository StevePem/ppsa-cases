Case Digest: Personal Property Securities Act 2009 (Australia)

The Personal Property Securities Act (the PPSA) was enacted in Australia in 2009 and came into full operation in 2012. It substantially reformed the law in Australia relating to security over personal property.

I have prepared a digest of cases that interpret or comment on the PPSA.
